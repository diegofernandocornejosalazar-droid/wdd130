Three ways to get out there

Choose your adventure

Gentle · Beginner friendly

Forest River Float

Settle into an easygoing paddle past shady banks and quiet swimming spots. Your guide shares local stories and helps you spot birds and other river wildlife along the way.

  • About 2.5 hours on the river
  • For ages 6 and up; no experience needed
  • Raft, paddle, helmet, and life jacket included

Action packed · Intermediate

Whitewater Run

Practice your paddle strokes with the crew, then take on a lively stretch of rolling rapids. Expect splashy drops, teamwork, and a riverside break to recharge.

  • About 4 hours, including a riverside break
  • For ages 12 and up; moderate fitness recommended
  • Raft, paddle, helmet, life jacket, and splash jacket included

Scenic · All experience levels

River & Lake Explorer

Enjoy a full day of changing scenery, from gentle river bends to wide-open water. Take time for a picnic, a swim when conditions allow, and an unhurried paddle back.

  • About 6 hours, including lunch and swim time
  • For ages 8 and up; no experience needed
  • Raft, paddle, helmet, life jacket, and dry bag included
